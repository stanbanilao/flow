How to read your business

This map is your company from the owner's point of view.

Start at Company and follow the connections. Each box represents a department, product, process, control point or system. The purpose is to make responsibilities, hand-offs, missing requirements and automation opportunities visible.

1

Start at Company

This is the full operating model. From here you can see how revenue, operations, customer service, people and technology connect.

2

Follow the arrows

The arrows show the hand-off: who receives the work next and where a delay or incomplete file can affect another department.

3

Tap a box

Tap or double-tap any box to see its purpose, owner, systems, minimum requirements and what should happen next.

4

Look for gaps

If a box has no clear owner, system, requirement or next step, that is normally a business-process gap worth fixing.

Five questions an owner should ask at every box

  • Who is accountable for this?
  • What must be received before work can start?
  • What proves this step is complete?
  • Which system or provider does the team rely on?
  • Can any repetitive part be automated without removing necessary human judgement?
