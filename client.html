How to use the map

Simple gestures only.

Single tap selects a box. Double-tap opens the explanation once. The explanation appears as a frosted-glass panel over the map. Swipe the small handle upward once to expand, or downward to collapse and close.

1

Single tap = select

The box gets a strong white outline so you can clearly see what you selected.

2

Double-tap = explain

You only double-tap once. The explanation opens without making you leave the map.

3

Swipe handle up = expand

Use the handle at the top of the glass panel. One upward swipe expands the explanation for reading.

4

Edit mode = move boxes

When Edit Layout is on, dragging boxes is for arranging the map and will not open explanations.
